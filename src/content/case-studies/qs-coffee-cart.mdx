## The problem

Q's Coffee Cart is a small business born out of the owner's addiction recovery
journey. The owner needed a simple website in order to promote their
business and take enquiries and bookings.

As a small startup, the goal was a simple site that didn't compromise on looks
while providing a fast experience and minimal ongoing costs. Ongoing updates
were going to be minimal, but the site needed to be ready for a content
management system in the future without a major rebuild.

## Constraints

- **Keep costs low in the early stages.** As a startup, we wanted to give as much
  profit back to the business.
- **A cool design without many existing assets.** The team had a logo, font and
  colour but little else to work with. We still needed a cool design aesthetic.
- **Take enquiries with as much info as possible.** The team are still working
  things out so we needed to figure out a form layout that would get them plenty
  of information to provide quotes without overcomplicating things.

## What I built

**We helped the team explore their initial design direction.** The team
needed to explore if the site should be led by Quincy's journey, or if it would
work better to lead with the business proposition. We provided a series of three
design, layout and content options to choose from with a round of edits and a
final draft to help them choose the best way forward.

Once a path was chosen, I got to work building the site using an Astro frontend.
Content is powered by JSON files and a middleware that would allow me to swap the
content for data supplied by a CMS later. Options include Sanity or WordPress
depending on their needs in the future.

The site code is tracked in GitHub, hosted through Cloudflare Workers and forms
are powered by Resend.com. This stack keeps ongoing costs to zero at this scale.
When the time comes to upgrade, running costs will start small and will scale.

## Stack

Astro and TypeScript for the site, Cloudflare Workers for hosting, Resend for
mail from the contact form.

## Results

- **Performance and accessibility are solid.** Lighthouse scores remain almost
  perfect, while accessibility is checked and tested to high standards.
- **SEO, OpenGraph and schema are all included.**
- **Content management ready** with an easy swap to a CMS for editing later on.
- **Ongoing costs are almost non-existent**, keeping early profits available for
  the growth of the business.
- **Full control given to Quincy**. All of the services have been created with
  Quincy as the primary account holder. I'm still on hand to help ongoing, but at
  all times the content and stack is fully owned by the team.
